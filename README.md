# Awesome Personal Agent Use Cases

Start with something you need to do. Each scenario shows a result, gives you a sample to try, and explains how to check it.

[简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) · [Builder guide](BUILDER.md) · [Sources and selection](EVIDENCE.md) · [Design](ARCHITECTURE.md) · [Contribute](CONTRIBUTING.md)

**Start with [What will I pay for next?](scenes/renewal-ledger.md)** Try making a renewal list from sample receipts and notices.

## Life

| Scenario | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| [Family schedule board · Who needs to be where this week?](scenes/household-calendar.md) | A schedule board Muse maintains for the whole family: school notices, group messages and shared calendars merged into one week view covering everyone's plans, changes, clashes and pickups — changes reach you for confirmation before anything hits the calendar. |
| [Subscription ledger · Tell me before I'm charged again](scenes/renewal-ledger.md) | A subscription ledger Muse keeps for you: what you subscribe to, which channel charges you, how much, when the next charge lands, and whether you're paying twice — it calls you before the money leaves, and keep-or-cancel is your call. |
| [Personal CRM · Friends and contacts](scenes/personal-crm.md) | A contacts library Muse maintains for you: one page per person covering channels, relationships and status, birthdays and greetings, and what you promised — plus a pre-meeting card before you meet. |
| [Repair records · Find who fixed it last time](scenes/home-repair.md) | Repair records Muse keeps for your home: each appliance's model, who fixed it last time, what it cost, and when the warranty ends — when something breaks, the last technician and work order are in front of you. |
| [Trip sheet · Every booking on one page](scenes/trip-brief.md) | A trip sheet Muse assembles for you: flight, hotel and transfer confirmations merged into one page sorted by local time, covering every leg's time, place and anything still unconfirmed — plus a dates-and-places-only copy for sharing. |
| [Price watch · Tell me when it fits my budget](scenes/price-watch.md) | A price watch Muse runs for you: you fix the conditions — what it is, which spec, the all-in price ceiling — and it checks on schedule, calling you only when an offer truly qualifies and staying silent otherwise. Buying is always your move. |

## Work

| Scenario | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| [Inbox triage · Which ones need my reply today?](scenes/inbox-triage.md) | A morning triage list Muse makes from your inbox: mail and messages sorted into reply today, read later, and waiting on material — each with its deadline, who is waiting on you, and what is missing. Replies are drafted first and sent only after you approve. |
| [File cabinet · Find that invoice months later](scenes/file-filing.md) | A file cabinet Muse keeps for invoices, receipts, warranties and contracts: originals stay untouched while it builds a searchable index — which merchant, which date, how much, and where the original lives. Possible duplicates are flagged, never merged. |
| [Pre-meeting card · Walk in with the context](scenes/meeting-prep.md) | A one-page card Muse prepares before each meeting: what was decided last time, what is still open, and the questions to ask this time — every line sourced, and proposals never dressed up as promises. |
| [Follow-up list · Who agreed to do what?](scenes/meeting-followups.md) | A follow-up list Muse extracts from meeting notes: what was decided, who promised what, by when, and which items still have no owner. It enters your task system only after you approve it, then it is tracked to the end. |
| [Morning action list · What actually needs me today?](scenes/daily-plan.md) | A morning action list Muse lays out daily: one main thread, at most three items, each with a start time, a next step and what it is blocked on. Waiting-on-others items sit in their own area, and the same list closes out at night — done, carried over, or dropped. |

## Learning

| Scenario | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| [Topic digest · Only what matters to this topic](scenes/reading-digest.md) | A short digest Muse filters by topic: you name the topic and the sources, and each issue carries only genuinely relevant new items — three at most, each with its original link and why it earned its place. Nothing new means one honest line, never padded old news. |
| [Study review · Quiz me from my own notes](scenes/study-review.md) | A review coach Muse runs from your own notes: it quizzes you only on material you have read, one question at a time with no answer up front, then marks you against the original — what matched, what you missed, which page to revisit. Half-missed questions come back later. |
| [Saved library · Find the thing I saved](scenes/reading-library.md) | A library Muse keeps of everything you save — articles, videos, posts — each stored with why you saved it and deduped by original source. Later you just ask in plain words, and it brings back the original together with your note. |
| [Evidence table · Trace every claim to its source](scenes/research-compare.md) | An evidence table Muse builds for one question: one row per claim — what was measured, under what conditions, and which passage says it. What the material never says is marked "not stated", and claims from different conditions are never forced into a ranking. |
